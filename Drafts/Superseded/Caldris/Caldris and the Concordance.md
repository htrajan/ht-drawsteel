# Caldris and the Concordance

> **SPOILER — Director layer.** Use this document to build Caldran locations, residents, return visits, and the Act 2 residency choice.
>
> **Tags:** INVENTED campaign setting + CANON-adjacent Timescape and quintessence.

---

## Foundational rule

**Caldris is a real parallel world.** Its people are not scenery, imitations, nonplayer projections, or incomplete versions of Orden people.

The Author calls it **the Shattered Margin** and regards it as a constructed editing environment. Caldrans call it **Caldris**. Neither name proves which world came first.

The Director truth is that both Caldris and Orden possess full quintessence, history, consciousness, and consequence. Even if one caused the other, creation does not make a world false.

---

## What feels familiar

- The same known ancestries live in Caldris. Devils, hakaans, humans, memonek, revenants, and other Orden ancestries are ordinary parts of society.
- Orden visitors can speak with Caldrans. Accent and idiom vary by region, but no translation device is required.
- Food nourishes, rain wets, stone breaks, injuries persist, and relationships matter.
- Political institutions, trade, performance, faith, and family life exist independently of the Author's project.
- An Orden visitor passes as a traveler from a distant Caldran region unless causal inconsistencies attract the Concordance.

Occasional people, customs, and events may **echo** Orden without mapping directly. Use a familiar mannerism in a stranger, an inverse civic event, or a melody with a different ending. Do not create direct copies of the heroes or their loved ones without explicit approval.

## What works differently

### Consensus governs access

Observation and agreement determine which version of a disputed place becomes easiest to reach. This is a practical civic problem handled by hearings, route witnesses, household records, and public bells.

Consensus does not prove dissenting histories false. A minority route can remain attached to a home, shrine, or family tradition.

### Contradiction has geography

Incompatible accounts can produce different stairs, rooms, streets, or seasons occupying the same region. Caldrans navigate these branches through custom and experience rather than treating them as constant emergencies.

### Revision needs a witness

Large changes require an unchanged reference capable of distinguishing before from after. The Author exploited this Caldran rule at the Gilded Lantern by leaving Dorian untouched on stage while erasing the audience.

### Relationships establish belonging

A person gains causal standing through witnessed choices, obligations, memories, and effects on other lives. This is why later visits can last longer: Caldran allies can temporarily attach the heroes to a local history.

## Grayfall

Ash transferred from Orden arrives as **grayfall**, a causally blank material accepted by Caldris without triggering its safeguards.

Common uses include:

- Glaze and black pigment.
- Orchard soil and roof gardens.
- Restoration mortar and replacement tile.
- Medicines whose efficacy varies by batch.
- Lampblack used in route records that must survive historical shifts.

Individual samples can contain shapes, sounds, preferences, or impressions that resemble memory. Never establish whether these are surviving people, copies, psychic residue, or pattern coincidence.

Caldran civilization is not populated primarily by recent Orden victims. Grayfall is one material within a much older world.

## Bell-Cairn

Bell-Cairn is a city built along both sides of a green, sunlit gorge. Bridges cross at several elevations; slate roofs collect rain and grayfall; civic bells coordinate public versions of disputed routes.

### Brackenrise

A terraced public rain garden above the city's east face. Waykeepers meet travelers, groundskeepers maintain white-barked orchards, and porters use bridges that open where a witnessed route expects a door.

### Grayfall Exchange

Roof-cistern samples are weighed and graded for craft, agricultural, and medical use. Traders are concerned with dampness, contamination, and price rather than metaphysics.

### Nine-Step Street

One side has eight landings and the other nine. Both serve the same homes until a route hearing determines which version public deliveries will use for the week.

### Garden of Two Dawns

Two ancient monuments record inverse accounts of Bell-Cairn and the hill clans opening their gates to one another after the Red Winter. Both show generations of weather and repair.

### Vesper Row and the Paired Theatre

Two theatres share one facade while occupying different performance histories. Their joined structure can hold several witnessed versions at once and served as the party's first route back to Orden.

## Waykeepers

Waykeepers are civic route witnesses, not wizards or secret police. They:

- Record which version of a public route a traveler used.
- Conduct hearings when roads, stairs, or addresses diverge.
- Help visitors find destinations attached to unfamiliar histories.
- Preserve minority routes when consensus would otherwise make them inaccessible.

**Sena Marr** works from Brackenrise and helped the heroes during Session 2. Her grandmother's route book contains older accounts of visitors surrounded by white Index Marks.

## The Concordance

The Concordance is Caldris's native consistency system. It is neither a government nor the Author's servant.

It recognizes a Caldran through causal history, not appearance. Orden visitors become detectable when they create **orphaned consequences**: changed objects, relationships, records, or routes with no Caldran chain leading to the person responsible.

### Visible escalation

1. Reflections lag or records omit a traveler's origin.
2. Footprints or touched objects acquire white Index Marks.
3. Indexing bodies reverse the visitor's recent consequences.
4. The White Cartographer verifies the foreign causes and attempts deletion.

The system ignores accounted-for residents. Residents can witness an attack without understanding why the apparently ordinary travelers were targeted.

### White Cartographer

The White Cartographer is an adaptive field intelligence. It speaks concisely, distinguishes residents from foreign objects, and seeks erasure rather than imprisonment or expulsion because it cannot verify an external destination.

Its vocabulary concerns causes, effects, routes, references, and accounting. It does not use the Author's masks, theatrical metaphors, or cult doctrine.

Destroying one manifestation ends that response, not the Concordance itself.

## Return visits

The first visit lasted only hours from the party's perspective and seconds in Orden. Unprotected future visits trigger detection more quickly because the Concordance now has the party's signatures.

Ways to extend a visit:

- A route held open by multiple Caldran witnesses.
- A temporary address recorded by a waykeeper.
- A Caldran obligation that depends on the heroes' continued presence.
- An object with an established local history willingly entrusted to them.
- Direct reclassification or negotiation with a White Cartographer manifestation.

These are temporary protections, not disguises that make Caldrans forget the truth.

---

## Permanent residency

At the Act 2 climax, the interface must be severed permanently. The party chooses its continuing home collectively.

To remain in Caldris, the heroes:

1. Cross before the interface closes.
2. Surrender the Orden tether that lets the other world extract them.
3. Ask Caldran witnesses to attach their continuing lives to local history.
4. Become valid Caldran causes through genuine shared consequence, not invented pasts.

To remain in Orden, they return and sever the interface from that side. Caldris continues beyond contact.

Neither choice destroys the unchosen world or proves it false. A hero who chooses separately receives an epilogue and leaves the active party.

## Design guardrails

- Lead Caldran scenes with daily life, beauty, work, humor, and curiosity.
- Danger comes from eventual Concordance detection or specific local conflicts, not from the realm being inherently hostile.
- Give Caldran residents goals unrelated to Orden and the Author.
- Let metaphysical rules create civic practices, relationships, exploration, and play—not only hazards.
- Keep the Author's editing project hidden inside a much larger living world.
- Preserve evidence for both cosmological readings; never turn “which world is real?” into a quiz with a correct player answer.
- Do not make every Caldran location a distorted Capital landmark.
- Do not resolve grayfall identity without explicit Director approval.
