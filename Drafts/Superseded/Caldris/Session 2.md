# Act 2, Session 2 — “The Shattered Margin”

> **Director run sheet:** A complete three-hour visit to Caldris for four level-3 heroes. The first seventy minutes are discovery without overt danger. Two Standard, objective-driven combats follow when Caldris detects the heroes as foreign causal objects. All required scene rules, tactical objectives, and creature statistics appear inline.
>
> **Player-facing prose rule:** Read-aloud text describes only what the heroes can perceive or recall from established play. Do not call Caldris a copy, simulation, construct, false world, or afterlife. NPCs speak from what they have witnessed; Director conclusions remain outside quotation marks.

## Session at a Glance

- **Expected length:** 3 hours, including a 10-minute buffer.
- **Heroes:** Level 3 — M.A.C. (Troubadour), Keth (Fury), Demona (Null), Dorian (Tactician).
- **Starting progression:** 4 XP and 4 Victories each.
- **Opening instant:** Vharos Kell has fallen in the Levitating Carpet Spooling Factory. He and every defeated cultist are becoming ash.
- **Structure:** Falling through the tear → ordinary life in Bell-Cairn → free discovery → the first deletion response → the Paired Theatre → escape from the White Cartographer.
- **Deliberate spotlights:** Dorian learns why the Gilded Lantern erasure left him standing; M.A.C. can establish that Caldris possesses genuine quintessence. Demona can read the living Orden signal, while Keth gets two mobile battlefields with clustered minions and terrain to break.
- **Maximum reward:** **2 Victories**, one for each combat objective.
- **No montage:** This session contains individual tests, open exploration, a cooperative discovery, and two combats. It contains zero montage tests.
- **Knowledge-only return:** The heroes' own equipment returns with them. Nothing acquired in Caldris survives the crossing.

### Director truth for this session

The people of Caldris call their world **Caldris**. **The Shattered Margin** is the Author's name for it, not theirs.

Caldris is a parallel reality with its own people, nations, history, and quintessence. The Author is a living psionic person physically located in Orden who uses a concealed interface to test changes in Caldris before imposing them on Orden. The Author sincerely regards Orden as real and Caldris as a constructed instrument.

The campaign's deeper truth is that both worlds and their inhabitants are fully real. Session 2 must not settle which came first. Orden-side evidence points to a living operator and an engineered interface; Caldran records and systems appear older than that interface.

The **Concordance** is Caldris's native consistency mechanism. Every Caldran person and object has a chain of causes reaching backward through local history. The heroes arrive intact and begin causing events without any Caldran past. The Concordance eventually identifies these **orphaned consequences** and attempts to erase the four foreign causes.

Ash crosses safely because the Author's interface strips away its prior connections and presents it as unclaimed material. The heroes retain their identities, memories, and Orden connections, so they do not pass the same test.

## Dramatis Personae

### Sena Marr — Bell-Cairn Waykeeper

- **Who:** A human route witness employed at the Brackenrise public garden. Waykeepers settle disputed streets, guide visitors, and record which version of a route a traveler actually used.
- **Appearance:** Copper route pins on a weatherproof green coat, cropped black hair, and an ink stain along the side of one thumb.
- **Motivation:** Find out why Bell-Cairn's own safeguards are hunting four apparently ordinary travelers without letting the answer cost more lives.
- **Demeanor:** Practical, sociable, and amused by tourists who pretend not to need directions. When frightened, she checks facts aloud and keeps her hands busy.
- **Limit:** Sena knows local history and waykeeping, not Orden, the Author, or the Concordance's inner workings.
- **Pitfall:** Treating Caldrans as copies, scenery, or incomplete people ends her cooperation. She does not need the heroes to believe her world came first; she needs them to recognize the people standing in it.
- **After detection:** Most witnesses flee or find an ordinary explanation. Sena stays because her grandmother's route book describes the same white marks around travelers who had no road behind them.

### The White Cartographer — Concordance Field Intelligence

- **Who:** An adaptive expression of Caldris's native consistency system. Its current body is a tall arrangement of white survey lines, joint marks, and empty space.
- **Purpose:** Remove foreign causes whose actions cannot be attached to Caldran history.
- **Demeanor:** Exact and responsive rather than theatrical. It answers relevant questions briefly, corrects false classifications, and continues the deletion process.
- **Not the Author:** It neither serves nor recognizes the Author's authority. The concealed interface has evaded it by feeding Caldris causally blank material.
- **This session:** It determines that the heroes have no Caldran antecedents and attempts to erase them. It does not threaten residents, punish helpers, or explain the campaign cosmology.

### Everyday Bell-Cairn Residents

Use ordinary people rather than lore dispensers:

- **Aro Fen**, a hakaan ash-glazier who buys roof-cistern grayfall by weight and complains cheerfully about damp batches.
- **Mell and Corren Thale**, devil siblings whose bakery occupies the eighth landing of Nine-Step Street on one history and the ninth on the other.
- **Old Namas**, a memonek groundskeeper who maintains both halves of the Garden of Two Dawns and refuses to favor either inscription.
- **Pava Seln**, a revenant costume mender at the Vesper Theatre who treats Dorian's corpse identity as unremarkable but admires his military stitching.

These people recognize the heroes' ancestries. M.A.C. might attract professional curiosity as a memonek, Dorian might be asked where he rose, and Demona's horns or Keth's height may earn a glance, but no one treats them as impossible.

## Scene 1 — Falling Upward

**Time:** 10 minutes.

**Purpose:** Continue directly from Kell's defeat, let the heroes act during the crossing, and reveal Caldris through sensory contrast rather than explanation.

Replace Session 1's closing before the tear seals.

> *Kell's smile goes first. His cheeks loosen into gray grains; his teeth tip backward through the empty place they leave. The same dust lifts from every fallen robe in the factory—not down to the boards, but sideways, streaming between the looms toward the pale slit at the rear wall.*
>
> *The slit widens. Daylight shows through it: wet green terraces, a strip of blue sky, rain climbing from leaves into a cloud below. The half-mask skates across the floor. Beneath it, the old playbill turns once, Dorian's name visible among the scratches, and both vanish into the opening.*
>
> *A shuttle follows. Then a broken chair. Warp threads snap from the looms and pull taut toward the same bright space. Boots slide. Loose coats lift. The brick floor stays level, but every unsecured thing in the room has begun to fall toward the wall.*

Ask: **What do you do?** Each hero gets one quick response before the current takes the party. Do not ask for a roll unless the result is uncertain and consequential.

Useful responses and benefits:

- **Keep the party together:** A hero who anchors, carries, commands, or coordinates chooses the party's landing positions in Scene 4.
- **Study the current:** A successful Reason or Intuition test grants an edge on one Grayfall Exchange or Paired Theatre test.
- **Hold the factory in mind:** A hero who deliberately fixes the room's arrangement in memory supplies one of the two required factory witnesses in Scene 6.
- **Protect equipment or another hero:** The protected target ignores the first forced movement or damage they would suffer in Scene 4.
- **Exploit a previously closed tear:** If the heroes closed the tear during Session 1, its briefly reopened seam is narrow and legible. Grant one automatic success on any one Paired Theatre test. Their earlier victory still mattered; it deprived Kell of the tear during combat and now gives them a cleaner crossing.

The pull cannot be escaped in this opening. A failed test changes landing or costs a surge; it does not leave one hero behind.

> *The factory narrows to a brick rectangle behind you. Gray dust pours past in a silent river. For a moment there is no up—only cold light, the tug of four different directions, and distant bells beating out a rhythm none of you know.*
>
> *Then wet grass meets you. Warm rain strikes your faces from an ordinary sky.*

## Scene 2 — Morning in Bell-Cairn

**Time:** 30 minutes.

**Purpose:** Give the players unpressured time to encounter a desirable, functioning parallel world. There is no attack, chase, countdown, warning, or overt threat in this scene.

### Arrival at Brackenrise

> *You lie among long green leaves beaded with rain. The garden descends in broad terraces toward a city built along both sides of a sunlit gorge. Slate roofs overlap like dark scales. Bridges cross the open air at half a dozen heights; one ends at a blank wall until a pair of porters approach, and then a doorway opens where the stone had been.*
>
> *Morning traffic moves along the paths around you. A devil in a yellow coat argues with a hakaan porter over a handcart. Two memonek couriers trade polished arm-plates beneath a striped awning. A revenant groundskeeper tips rainwater from the empty socket of one glass eye. He looks at Dorian, taps two fingers to his brow in greeting, and returns to his roses.*
>
> *A woman in a green coat waits for Keth to move off the flowerbed before pointing to a gravel path. “You'll want the paving. Brackenrise charges for crushed valerian, and Namas can identify every boot that did it.”*

Stop. Let the players look, question, test, or simply walk. The woman is **Sena Marr**, on duty as a waykeeper. She assumes the heroes are travelers from another Caldran region.

If asked where they are:

> *Sena glances toward the city as if checking whether the question might mean the garden or the district. “Brackenrise, above the east face of Bell-Cairn.” She points across the gorge. “Crown stairs are that way. Grayfall Exchange is down three terraces. If you mean the country, Caldris.”*

If asked about Orden, the Shattered Margin, or the Unwritten Dark, she tries each name once and shakes her head. She is not withholding information.

If the heroes describe arriving through a tear, Sena looks back at the undamaged lawn, the paths, and the cloud cover. She asks where the opening is now. There is nothing visible to show her.

### Establish ordinary life

Let the party choose their pace. Use two or three of these brief interactions before Sena offers directions:

- Aro Fen asks whether the heroes are waiting to sell a grayfall claim; his apprentice has already weighed the fresh dust in the roof cistern.
- A child asks M.A.C. whether his chassis is Caelian work, then loses interest when a pastry cart passes.
- Pava Seln notices a split seam in Dorian's coat and offers the name of a revenant tailor rather than commenting on his body.
- A street musician plays a four-beat phrase that resembles a Capital canal call, then resolves it in a different key.
- A public notice lists two dates for tomorrow's bridge closure and instructs travelers to use whichever date appears on their household calendar.

The heroes can eat the food, feel the rain, break a twig, or examine a building. Nothing is translucent, dreamlike, or insubstantial. Do not call attention to this unless a player investigates.

### Sena's four suggestions

When the party wants direction, Sena names four places without turning them into a quest list:

1. **Grayfall Exchange**, where the fresh ash is already being traded.
2. **Nine-Step Street**, where a public route hearing is drawing a crowd.
3. **The Garden of Two Dawns**, the oldest open ground nearby.
4. **Vesper Row**, where Bell-Cairn's paired theatres are changing programs.

The next scene lasts thirty minutes. Let the heroes choose two or three places. Sena accompanies them if invited; otherwise she gives clear directions and reappears naturally at the route hearing or after the first combat.

### First quiet discrepancies

After the heroes have made several choices, introduce one detail and move on without emphasis:

- A patch of fogged glass reflects the passing crowd immediately; the heroes' reflections arrive one step later.
- A ticket punch leaves four clean holes in the card but prints no station names beside them.
- Mud fills in one hero's footprint from the heel forward while surrounding prints remain.

No local notices unless a hero points it out. A resident offers an ordinary explanation—cheap glass, a worn punch, thirsty soil—and continues their day.

## Scene 3 — A World at Work

**Time:** 30 minutes.

**Purpose:** Let the heroes discover Caldris's metaphysical rules through civic life. Core observations require no roll. Tests add precision, useful advantages, or personal insight; failure causes curiosity or inconvenience, never danger.

Visit at least two locations. If the players linger, the first combat begins wherever they are at 1:10.

### Grayfall Exchange

> *Copper gutters carry gray water from the roofs into a row of stone cisterns. Traders dip long glass tubes into each one, hold the samples to the sun, and argue over color. The newest basin still turns with a slow silver current. Flecks of ash cling to its sides above chalk marks for pottery, orchard soil, lampblack, and medicine.*
>
> *Aro Fen lifts a wet sample on one thumb. For a breath, a black curl like a letter appears in the paste. He smears it flat, adds another scoop to the pottery measure, and calls for the next seller.*

Freely observable:

- The fresh grayfall matches Kell's and the cultists' ash in color, temperature, and faint stage-dust smell.
- Caldrans regard it as a useful but inconsistent natural resource.
- No identifiable person or intact memory emerges from it.

Possible investigations:

| Approach | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Demona follows the psionic resonance | On 12–16, she finds a faint pulse traveling against the ash current. On 17+, it has the irregular rhythm of a living mind rather than a repeating spell. On ≤11, the signal is lost among thousands of local impressions, but she still confirms the ash came through the same channel as the party. |
| M.A.C. examines the material | On 12–16, its quintessence is native to Caldris despite the Orden resonance embedded in it. On 17+, both signatures are complete; neither resembles an illusion or summoned imitation. On ≤11, the signatures cannot be separated, but both are present. |
| Any hero asks what grayfall becomes | Aro shows glaze, roof tile, black pigment, and pear trees rooted in ash-rich soil. He has never seen it make a person. |

A hero who preserves a sample can carry it during the session. It dissolves during the return crossing.

### Nine-Step Street

> *The street climbs between two rows of narrow houses. On the left, nine landings rise from the canal to the clock arch. On the right, there are eight. Both sides meet the same doorways. A baker leans from an upper window and lowers a basket to customers standing on two different steps in the same patch of sunlight.*
>
> *At the foot of the hill, six neighbors hold a blue cord between them while a waykeeper reads yesterday's deliveries aloud. With each answer, one stair sharpens and another goes smooth at the edges.*

Freely observable:

- Both versions function until the hearing settles which route public services will use for the week.
- Agreement does not prove one history false; it chooses which version remains easy to enter.
- Private homes can remain attached to the less-used version.

The heroes can watch or participate. If one supplies a relevant observation, no test is needed—their statement changes a step like anyone else's. Immediately afterward, the waykeeper's brass tally records a vote with no name, address, or prior route beside it. The clerk assumes the stylus skipped.

A Reason, Intuition, or Presence test can examine or influence the process:

- **≤11:** The hero reaches the intended landing but exits through the opposite bakery. Mell Thale gives them a hot roll for the inconvenience.
- **12–16:** The hero can choose which version they occupy until leaving the street.
- **17+:** The hero holds both versions in view at once. Grant an edge on one Paired Theatre test.

### Garden of Two Dawns

> *Two stone arches face the same pond. The eastern arch records that Bell-Cairn opened its gates after the Red Winter and welcomed the hill clans inside. The western arch records that the hill clans opened their gates and admitted Bell-Cairn. Lichen fills both sets of letters. Tree roots have lifted both foundations.*
>
> *Between them, families picnic beneath white-barked trees. No fence divides the monuments. Old Namas sweeps petals from one inscription, crosses the grass, and gives the other the same care.*

Freely observable:

- Both monuments show generations of repair, weather, and use.
- Local family names appear on both, with different roles.
- Neither inscription references the Author, Orden, or a moment when Caldris began.

Possible investigations:

- **History or Reason:** A 17+ result finds repairs dated earlier than the oldest Orden-side erasure the party knows. A lower result still establishes that the monuments were not recently staged.
- **M.A.C. and Timescape lore:** No roll is required to establish the core fact: Caldris carries quintessence as completely as Orden. A 17+ result identifies two consistent historical strata occupying the same space without either being a psychic projection.
- **Physical examination:** Keth or any hero who cuts, lifts, tastes, or breaks something finds ordinary material consequences. Namas objects only if they damage the memorial.

### Vesper Row

> *The theatre district announces itself by laundry: painted seas, palace walls, and twenty-foot sections of night sky hanging from lines between the roofs. Two playhouses share one facade. The left doors advertise a comedy that closed last year. The right doors sell tickets for its opening night tomorrow.*
>
> *A costume mender sits between them with a jacket spread across her knees. When Dorian passes, she looks over the old stitching at his shoulder. “Military hand,” she says. “Good work. Miserable lining.” She offers him her card.*

Pava Seln is a revenant and treats Dorian as another revenant. If he discusses theatre, she describes the paired houses: each preserves a different history of the same company, and actors sometimes cross during performances. A scratched Gilded Lantern playbill or the name of Dorian's old play does not exist here.

A hero who studies the joined facade notices one structural point shared by both buildings: a single seat or stage position kept unchanged while everything around it differs. This foreshadows the Paired Theatre without explaining Dorian's survival.

### The accumulated discrepancy

At the hard stop, use the location the players currently occupy.

> *A white line appears around the heel of the hero who last touched something. It is no wider than chalk. It follows the edge of the footprint, continues along every print behind it, and stops at the place where your first step in Bell-Cairn should have begun.*
>
> *There is no beginning there.*
>
> *Three more lines draw themselves across stone, soil, and stair. Around the four of you, every path ends in a clean white circle.*

Do not explain what this means. Give the players one brief response, then begin Scene 4.

## Scene 4 — The World Notices

**Time:** 35 minutes; maximum four rounds.

**Purpose:** Turn accumulated curiosity into the first deletion response. The entities target only the heroes and the consequences they created. Residents remain physically real, tactically present, and beneath the response's notice.

### Arrival of the response

> *The nearest white circle folds upward. It has no thickness. Survey marks hinge into long legs; blank intervals form a rib cage around open air. Across the street, smaller figures pull themselves from the lines around a handprint, a dropped coin, the corner of a ticket.*
>
> *One reaches toward a cup a hero moved earlier. The cup retraces its fall, climbs onto the vendor's tray, and seals along the crack. The vendor's hand closes on an object that is no longer broken. The white figure turns from him without pause.*
>
> *Every empty face turns toward you.*

Residents scatter, take cover, or stand stunned. The creatures never target a resident, even when a resident attacks them. Sena is present or arrives during round 1; she takes cover and watches what the marks remove.

### Tactical layout

Use a **20 × 16-square** map adapted to the current location.

- **Three Index Marks:** Place one 5–8 squares from each other at three sites the heroes affected. Each is an object with Stamina 12, Stability 0, and immunity poison and psychic.
- **Terraces:** Two platforms stand 2 squares above the main street, reached by stairs or a climb. They provide high ground and half-height walls.
- **Awning lanes:** Three 2 × 4 market awnings provide cover. A hero can cut a support as a maneuver, dropping a 2 × 2 patch that restrains creatures beneath it until they escape with a medium Might or Agility test.
- **Split stair:** A 2-square-wide stair occupies two positions at once. A creature entering it chooses either exit; forced movement chooses randomly.
- **Civic bell:** A hanging bell can be struck with an attack or maneuver. Until the start of the striker's next turn, one chosen Index Mark loses all immunities and attacks against it gain an edge.
- **Residents:** Treat fleeing residents as blocking terrain until the end of round 1. A hero can direct a group to safety with a maneuver; no test is required unless the route is dangerous.

### Disabling an Index Mark

A hero can damage a mark normally or use a maneuver while adjacent to attempt a **medium test** appropriate to their method:

- **Might or Agility:** Break, obscure, rotate, or physically cross the lines.
- **Reason or Intuition:** Trace the impossible route and supply a missing local cause.
- **Presence:** Get two Caldrans to witness and name the hero's action, attaching it to the current moment.

| Power roll | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **≤11** | The mark remains. The hero takes 3 psychic damage as their last footstep vanishes from the street. |
| **12–16** | Disable the mark. The hero is slowed until the end of their next turn as the line removes the space beneath their stride. |
| **17+** | Disable the mark. The hero can shift up to 3 squares or grant an edge to the next attempt against a different mark. |

A disabled or destroyed mark cannot be repaired in this encounter.

### Four-round clock

- **Round 1:** The response removes minor objects the heroes moved.
- **End of round 2:** Each active Index Mark erases the hero's shadow within 5 squares of it. That hero has a bane on tests made to disable that mark until another hero witnesses them with a maneuver.
- **End of round 3:** White lines spread across every exit. The street becomes difficult terrain for the heroes; enemies ignore it.
- **End of round 4:** Resolve the first deletion pass, then end the encounter. Each hero within 5 squares of an active mark makes a **medium Intuition test**: ≤11: 8 psychic damage and weakened (save ends); 12–16: 4 psychic damage; 17+: no effect. The remaining response bodies collapse into loose lines after the pulse.

If all three marks are disabled before the end of round 4, end the encounter immediately. Award **1 Victory**. For each mark still active when the deletion pass resolves, the final encounter begins with **2 additional Malice**.

## Scene 5 — The Paired Theatre

**Time:** 25 minutes.

**Purpose:** Discover a route home, reveal why Dorian survived, establish that the Orden operator is alive, and preserve uncertainty about which reality came first.

### Entering both histories

Sena takes the heroes through service alleys to the joined theatres on Vesper Row. White marks begin appearing at intersections behind them, but nothing attacks before the scene's hard stop.

> *The left playhouse smells of dust and oranges. Rows of covered seats descend to a bare stage. Through the open wall on your right, the same room is crowded for tomorrow's performance: lamps lit, musicians tuning, stagehands carrying a painted moon overhead.*
>
> *The two rooms share one proscenium arch. When Sena stands in the left aisle, the right-hand theatre fades. When she crosses to the right, dust sheets settle over the empty seats behind her. From the threshold, you can see both.*

The way forward is a cooperative discovery, not a montage. Each task uses a separate hero and can be solved with a strong fictional action without a roll.

1. **Keep both Caldran theatres present.** Two heroes stand where they can independently witness each version or use abilities that preserve both. If uncertain, use medium Reason, Intuition, or Presence tests. Failure costs 3 psychic damage or time but does not close the route permanently.
2. **Introduce the Gilded Lantern memory.** Dorian can step onto the shared stage and describe one concrete feature he remembers. If Dorian refuses or cannot act, another hero can use the playbill image from the crossing or recount what Dorian established in Session 1. Do not invent Dorian's feelings or dialogue.
3. **Trace the third geometry.** Once both Caldran histories and the Orden memory are held at once, any hero can follow the new lines with Reason, Intuition, Psionics, Magic, or a relevant class feature.

### What appears

> *A third set of seats shows through the other two—not solid, no more than pale edges laid across the room. Timber galleries cut through Bell-Cairn stone. A hundred and twenty narrow chairs face the stage.*
>
> *Fine lines rise from every chair. They gather above the audience, bend inward, and pass through a dark opening high over the house. One place is not connected to them: a pool of stage light, the width of a man standing with his arms spread.*
>
> *At its center, letters press briefly through the boards from below.*
>
> ***REFERENCE RETAINED: DORIAN ASHVEIL.***

Stop and let the players interpret it. If they ask for confirmation, a successful test is not required for the core conclusion: the audience revision used Dorian's unchanged observation as its reference. He was deliberately left standing because the change needed a witness. The geometry does not show that the Author planned his later murder or revenant return.

### Orden signal

The dark opening above the spectral Gilded Lantern is the route used by the Author's interface.

- **Demona or another psionic read:** The signal includes breath, pulse, momentary distraction, and the pressure changes of a living body. It originates on the Orden side of the connection. A 17+ result also catches distant Capital bells, but no district or identity.
- **M.A.C. or Timescape reasoning:** The channel connects two complete quintessence systems. Caldris is not a projected image inside the operator's mind. A 17+ result shows the interface entering an older Caldran structure rather than generating the structure around itself.
- **Historical comparison:** Sena's route book describes travelers with no roads behind them three generations before the oldest date carved into the interface housing. The book can be wrong, and the interface could have been rebuilt. It is evidence, not proof.

Do not reveal the Author's name, face, location, or motive. The heroes can establish only that a living Orden mind operates the channel and that the channel does not explain all of Caldris.

### Preparing the route

The joined theatre exposes three pieces needed to return:

- **Witness Dais:** Holds an agreed image of the factory destination.
- **Ash Flywheel:** Carries grayfall into Caldris and can be reversed.
- **Shared Stage Door:** Opens only after the destination and current agree.

Sena can identify their local functions. She has never used them to reach another world.

At the hard stop, the building goes quiet. Read:

> *The musicians in the occupied theatre stop one by one. Not together—the nearest first, then the row behind, then the next, silence moving away from you like a tide.*
>
> *White measurements descend from the rafters. They mark the height of the doors, the distance between each of you, the exact place where every footprint begins. A tall outline steps down from the empty numbers and settles onto the stage.*
>
> *Its face is a blank grid. The lines turn toward Sena, then away. Toward the actors, then away. They stop on the four of you.*
>
> *“Four effects,” it says. “No causes found.”*

Allow one short exchange before initiative.

Useful Cartographer answers:

- **What are you?** “I keep what happened attached to what caused it.”
- **Why us?** “You begin after your first consequence.”
- **Can we leave?** “There is no prior place within Caldris to return you to.”
- **We came from Orden.** “No such antecedent is present.”
- **Will you hurt the others?** “They are accounted for.”

It will not accept exile as a solution because it cannot verify an external destination. When the heroes continue toward the mechanism, it begins deletion.

## Scene 6 — Deletion at the Root

**Time:** 35 minutes; maximum five rounds.

**Purpose:** Complete a route to Orden while fighting Caldris's adaptive deletion response. This is a Standard encounter with an objective ending, not a battle to decide whether Caldris deserves to exist.

### Tactical layout

Use a **22 × 16-square** theatre map.

- **Shared stage:** An 8 × 6 platform, 1 square above the orchestra floor. The White Cartographer begins at center stage.
- **Two auditoriums:** The west half is empty and dust-covered; the east half contains seats and abandoned instruments. Seats are difficult terrain and provide cover while crouched.
- **Balconies:** Two 2-square-high balconies run along the north and south walls. Ladders, curtains, or a medium Agility test reach them.
- **Witness Dais:** Two lecterns stand on opposite balconies. Each must receive one success from a different hero.
- **Ash Flywheel:** A 2 × 2 mechanism beneath a trapdoor at the front of the stage. Reaching it costs 2 additional squares of movement unless the trapdoor is broken or opened with a maneuver.
- **Shared Stage Door:** A freestanding door at the rear of the stage. It can be activated only after both lecterns and the flywheel are complete.
- **Scene lifts:** Three 2 × 2 platforms rise or descend 2 squares when a creature uses an adjacent lever as a maneuver.
- **Paired seam:** A diagonal white line divides the two histories. It is the encounter's dynamic hazard.

### Opening the route

Progress cannot be reversed once earned. A hero can attempt each task with any ability or approach that addresses the fiction.

#### 1. Hold the factory image — two witnesses

Two different heroes must each use a maneuver at opposite Witness Daises and make a **medium Reason, Intuition, or Presence test**. A hero who fixed the factory in mind during Scene 1 supplies one automatic success.

- **≤11:** No success; take 3 psychic damage.
- **12–16:** One success; the hero is slowed until the end of their next turn.
- **17+:** One success; the hero or one ally can shift up to 3 squares.

#### 2. Reverse the ash current

A hero adjacent to the Ash Flywheel uses a main action and a **medium Might, Agility, Reason, or Intuition test**. Psionics, machinery, forced movement, or an appropriate heroic ability can solve it without a roll.

- **≤11:** The current remains inward; take 5 psychic damage and slide 3 toward the flywheel.
- **12–16:** Reverse the current.
- **17+:** Reverse the current, and the White Cartographer takes 10 damage that cannot be reduced.

#### 3. Open the Shared Stage Door

Once the two witnesses and flywheel are complete, a hero adjacent to the door uses a main action and makes a **medium test** suited to how they join the destination to the reversed current. Dorian can use his status as the Gilded Lantern's retained witness, but no class or hero is required.

- **≤11:** The door remains shut; the hero takes 5 psychic damage.
- **12–16:** The route opens.
- **17+:** The route opens, and each hero can immediately shift up to 3 squares toward it.

When the route opens, finish the current hero's turn and let every hero describe how they cross. End the encounter without requiring movement bookkeeping. If the White Cartographer is reduced to 0 Stamina first, its body collapses and the paired histories supply one automatic success to every incomplete task; Sena helps finish the route and the heroes escape.

### Five-round clock

At the end of each round, resolve the Paired Theatre hazard. At the end of round 5, if the route is not open and the Cartographer still stands, the surviving Orden connection tears the heroes out before deletion completes:

- Each hero takes 10 psychic damage that cannot reduce them below 1 Stamina.
- The heroes return to Orden with all core discoveries.
- The Bell-Cairn route closes.
- Sena's assistance is indexed; she must abandon her waykeeper post before a future visit.

Award **1 Victory** if the party opens the route or defeats the White Cartographer before extraction.

## Scene 7 — Where the Dust Hasn't Landed

**Time:** 5 minutes, followed by the reserved 10-minute buffer.

Read after a successful route opening:

> *The stage door opens onto the factory wall. The brick hall beyond is exactly where you left it: snapped warp threads still recoiling, the same shuttle a handspan above the floor, the last of Kell's ash not yet settled.*
>
> *Behind you, Sena stands between two versions of the theatre. The closing doorway narrows her first to a green coat, then to one copper route pin catching the light. Bell-Cairn's morning bells sound once through the gap.*
>
> *Your boots strike factory boards. The door becomes a pale seam, then brick. The shuttle hits the floor.*

On forced extraction, omit Sena at the doorway. The heroes hit the factory floor as white lines briefly trace their bodies and vanish.

### What returns

- Every hero and all equipment they brought from Orden.
- Their memories of Caldris, Sena, the grayfall, and the Paired Theatre.
- Knowledge that a living mind on the Orden side operates the interface.
- Knowledge that Caldris has ordinary people, history, and complete quintessence.
- Knowledge that Dorian was deliberately retained as the Gilded Lantern revision's unchanged witness.

No Caldran sample, route book, coin, card, or other acquired object survives.

### What remains unresolved

- The Author's identity and physical location.
- Whether Orden or Caldris existed first.
- Whether grayfall preserves victims, copies impressions, or merely carries usable matter.
- Whether the White Cartographer can be reasoned with on a later visit.
- How to remain in Caldris without eventually being detected.

### Record after play

- Which Bell-Cairn locations the heroes visited and whom they spoke with.
- What each hero did that produced an Index Mark.
- How many marks survived the first encounter.
- Whether the route opened normally or extraction triggered.
- Whether Sena kept her waykeeper post.
- The heroes' first conclusions about which world is real; do not correct them.
- Award up to **2 Victories** now, after both encounters.

### Deferred Orden threads

The Ash Ledger investigation remains available for a later Act 2 session: Melvin Corbin's compromised Peacekeepers' Office, Oria Venn's forged transfer, Nera Kell, Sabine Corda, the Blue-Wax contractors, and Vesk's memorandum are unchanged. They can eventually identify the Author's physical infrastructure in Orden. Do not begin that investigation during this coda.

## Director Audit Before Play

- **Runtime:** 10 + 30 + 30 + 35 + 25 + 35 + 5 + 10 buffer = **180 minutes**.
- **Danger timing:** No overt threat, warning, pursuit, or hostile creature appears before the 70-minute mark.
- **Montages:** Exactly zero.
- **Combats:** EV 40 and EV 50; four- and five-round hard stops; objective endings.
- **Creature limits:** Maximum level 5; 19 creatures maximum; at least half are minions; no more than four stat blocks in either encounter.
- **Rewards:** Maximum 2 Victories.
- **Dorian:** Learns why he remained, not who commissioned the erasure or who the Author is.
- **Cosmology:** Both realities display genuine history and quintessence. No evidence proves either is the original.
- **Return:** Only knowledge and Orden-origin equipment cross back.
- **Prose:** Every read-aloud block contains observable details only. Spoken lines arise from the speaker's immediate role and evidence. No player-facing text calls Caldris false, artificial, a draft, or disposable.
