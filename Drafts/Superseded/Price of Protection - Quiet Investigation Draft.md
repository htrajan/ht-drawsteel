Anselmo stops at a covered-market food stall before returning to the office. If the heroes already approached him or bypassed the market, move this conversation to the plausible location rather than making him replay it.

## Scene 5 — The Accounts Wallet (1:25–2:05)

He wants the site secured, its remaining stores accounted for, and the contractor's books out of investigators' hands. ### Quiet arrival — do not read the confrontation unless detected

> *Two cargo carpets hover above the loading floor. Through the open office window, the man in the brown coat lays his wallet on a desk. A grey-haired captain turns it toward the lamp.*
>
> *“Here's the bill for the locksmith and the wagon at the factory,” the clerk says. “I need you to sign it.”*
>
> *The captain signs, counts a pouch of money, and passes the wallet back.*

From a suitable established vantage, the heroes can see this without revealing themselves. Eavesdrop or reaching a more exposed position is only a test if their plan leaves a real risk. Do not require a last arbitrary detection roll after a successful approach.

Before they choose to enter, describe the guards and hired mages their vantage reveals, using the contingency roster for actual numbers and positions. They guard Belmonte and the contractor's depot office; they are not relocated factory sentries. A low outcome changes whether those people notice the party, not whether they appear as surprise reinforcements.

### Three complete noncombat routes

| Route | What the players arrange | Proof and exit |

**Other plans:** Buying information, leveraging a genuine authority, a relevant class ability, or watching a transaction closely enough to secure verifiable testimony can work. Honor an ability's actual reach and limitations; NPCs gain no improvised immunity to keep combat on the schedule.

### If discretion is uncertain

Before rolling, state the immediate risk: who can see the heroes, what looks suspicious, and what being caught would mean. **Scenario procedure:** Resolve one coordinated approach with one lead hero's test for its exposed action; the other heroes explain their support or choose separate tasks. This is not the core group-test procedure. Do not impose four independent chances to trigger a fight simply because four heroes participate. Separate tests require genuinely separate risks, not repeated checks of the same cover story.

**Scenario outcome table for getting and leaving with the proof:**

| Power roll | Proof | Detection and next choice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **17+** | Secure the intended record or copy | Leave unnoticed or with the cover intact; **do not start combat** |
| **12–16** | Secure the record or copy | A sergeant catches the exposed handoff or stops them at the exit. Play the challenge below; a credible explanation, negotiated concession, or clean withdrawal can still avoid fighting |
| **≤11** | The attempt is interrupted before the missing record is secured; earlier evidence remains | Belmonte identifies an intrusion and blocks the obvious exit. He demands the papers. Resolve an offered way out; if the heroes refuse and no credible cover or agreement remains, begin the conditional combat |

These are bespoke scenario outcomes, not a claim that every medium test in the core rules gives those consequences. Natural 19–20 still grants the normal critical test reward: a helpful office contact or the next verification step, not a forced encounter.

**Suspicion follows facts, not a hidden meter.** If Anselmo noticed a tail in Scene 3, he can describe or point out that follower to the office guards once he reaches them. They did not witness the factory scene themselves, and an unseen ally is not magically recognized. A disguise, different entrance, convincing explanation, or actual withdrawal changes what they know. Do not erase prior suspicion on a high roll without explaining how the action addressed it.

### The challenge — dialogue before initiative

For a suspicious but unconfirmed visitor:

> *The sergeant steps between you and the stairs. “That isn't your paperwork. Who told you to take it?”*

For a detected intrusion:

> *Belmonte closes the office door behind him. “Put the papers on the desk. You're leaving without them.”*

Allow an answer. A credible cover based on real details can settle the challenge. A negotiated exit can involve returning the **original** while retaining an agreed copy, allowing Anselmo to verify only the relevant entries, or leaving with already-recorded observations. Belmonte may accept a believable mundane explanation to avoid a public dispute; he does not automatically agree to publish incriminating records. Anselmo, not Belmonte, is the stronger route to willing evidence.

If uncertain, one Presence/Reason test: **17+** accepts the credible exit terms; **12–16** lets them leave with a named concession consistent with what was offered (for example leaving originals, not erasing knowledge); **≤11** rejects the proposal. A failed proposal alone does not force them to attack: they can surrender papers and leave, accepting any actual evidence loss. If they refuse to relinquish the disputed evidence and cannot get out unnoticed, Belmonte orders his crew to take it. **That is the combat trigger.** Never turn a peaceful high outcome into “he attacks anyway.”

### Branch parity

- **Quiet success:** The two findings secured and delivered earn **2 Victories** and the full fee: **+1 Wealth to each hero**. Belmonte can remain alive, employed, and unaware of the inquiry.
- **Detected success without combat:** The same two findings earn **2 Victories** and the same fee: **+1 Wealth to each hero**. Record whose face or cover was exposed.
- **Detected success with combat:** Recover the two findings and escape or prevail: **2 Victories** and the same fee, **+1 Wealth to each hero**. No bonus for kills or capturing Belmonte.
- **Partial investigation:** One verified finding earns **1 Victory** and half the monetary fee, whatever happened in a fight; **no Wealth increase yet**. Credit that installment toward completion, not a fractional score.
- **No verified finding:** No Victory, Wealth increase, or success fee; reimburse agreed expenses, retain genuine leads, and discuss a follow-up rather than inventing success.

### Rules at this battlefield

- **Initiative:** Heroes and enemy groups alternate turns; heroes choose their own order. Every creature acts once per round. A group resolves all its members together. No allies add combat turns or encounter strength; their help is off-map.
- **Malice:** Start this encounter with the party's average current Victories (round down). At the start of each round, add living heroes plus round number: normally +5, +6, +7, +8, +9. Discard unspent Malice after this encounter. Use only the printed abilities and Human Malice options below; there are no hidden reinforcements.
- **Potency and duration:** M/A/R/I/P refer to Might/Agility/Reason/Intuition/Presence; a condition after “M < 2,” for example, applies only if Might is below 2. EoT ends at the end of the target's next turn, or its current turn if imposed during that turn. Save ends: roll d10 at each turn's end; 6+ ends the effect.
- **Relevant conditions:** Taunted gives a double bane on ability rolls that do not target the taunter while they are within line of effect. Slowed caps speed at 2 and prevents shifting. Restrained sets speed to 0, prevents Stand Up and forced movement, gives a bane on ability rolls and Might/Agility tests, and grants abilities against the creature an edge; teleporting ends it. Prone gives a bane on strikes, grants melee strikes against the creature an edge, and makes crawling cost 1 extra square; Stand Up is a maneuver. Grabbed sets speed to 0, prevents Knockback and forced movement except by its source, and gives a bane on abilities not targeting the grabber. The source brings its captive when it moves; its speed is halved if the captive is at least its size. The grab ends if released, the target teleports, or forced movement separates the pair. Conditions do not stack with themselves.
- **Recovery:** Catch Breath is a maneuver: spend one of the hero's own Recoveries to regain their recovery value in Stamina. A dying hero cannot Catch Breath. There is no automatic healing between scenes.
- **Free strikes:** These monsters deal their listed fixed Free Strike damage, without a roll; range is their signature's range or melee 1/ranged 5, whichever is greater. An opportunity attack is a melee free strike when an adjacent enemy willingly moves away without shifting or teleporting. Forced movement does not provoke. A monster with a bane against that target cannot make the opportunity attack.
- **Positioning:** Cover exists when a solid object blocks at least half the target and imposes a bane on damage-dealing abilities against it. Difficult terrain costs 1 extra square to enter. Stability reduces horizontal forced movement by its value, minimum 0. Falling 2+ squares deals 2 damage per square and leaves the creature prone; reduce effective height by Agility, minimum 0. Use stairs and ladders at 1 extra square of movement per square climbed unless the creature has a climb speed.
- **Captures:** A hero reducing a creature to 0 Stamina can knock it unconscious rather than kill it. Do not require lethal damage to end an encounter. Do not attack downed heroes for convenience.

**Minions, fully inline:** Each color is a separate squad of four with its own pool. All members share one signature roll when attacking. Each target takes one attack result; a second or third minion on that same target adds its Free Strike damage (at most three attackers per target). Each minion has a move and a main action, a move and a maneuver, or two moves—not all three. A minion using a personal maneuver does not join the squad attack. A critical hit gives each participating minion a free strike.

Reduce a squad's pool by damage suffered. At every multiple of individual Stamina lost, remove one minion. A non-area hit can remove additional nearest squad members after the struck one; let the player narrate it. An area ability damages the pool once per affected minion, but cannot kill minions outside its area; cap that area damage at individual Stamina times affected members before immunity/weakness. Apply a squad's immunity or weakness only once to each damage source, not once per affected minion. Minions cannot regain or gain temporary Stamina and cannot be winded. Simultaneous free strikes from one squad into one target are one strike with summed damage.

Attach the named captain to its colored squad; keep the captain's Stamina separate. While that captain lives, the squad gains its printed With Captain bonus. A captain leads only one squad. A replacement non-minion can take command at the start of the next round. All squad members and their captain activate in the same initiative group.

**Human features below are book rules, not cult evidence.** Supernatural Insight ignores supernatural concealment; it does not read minds, detect lies, or defeat mundane cover. No monster gains additional unprinted abilities.
