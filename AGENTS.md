# Campaign writing

Before creating or revising campaign material, read and follow [Campaign/Soul.md](Campaign/Soul.md).

Player-facing explanations must be ready to deliver as NPC dialogue, read-aloud flavor text, or a readable handout. Do not leave them buried in verbose GM background notes. Keep GM notes for secrets and running the scene, retain complete necessary mechanics, and audit the player-facing text on its own for clarity and appropriate character knowledge.

Current user instructions and established played history take precedence over defaults and unplayed session plans.
