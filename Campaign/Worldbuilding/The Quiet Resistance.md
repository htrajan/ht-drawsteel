# The Quiet Resistance

> **Tags:** INVENTED NPCs placed on published factions (Far Mariners, the Font, House Navarr) and the INVENTED Bureau of Accounts.
>
> **Use when:** The party tries to involve authorities, seeks allies after the Reckoning, or you want a side character to **become important** (Lesley's request).

> **Current Session 2:** [The Price of Protection](../Act%202/Session%202.md): Corbin's warning, limited access to a Gold Buttons office, five assignments, the Dog-leg's missing repairs, and Bellafonte's animated collection. Other contacts below are optional; no Font commission or patron audience is scheduled. Only Melvin is already confirmed met.

## Design intent

Capital's rot is **systemic** — the party should **feel** that reporting crimes fails before they find people who **already knew** and kept fighting anyway. Resistance NPCs are:

- **Competent but constrained** — they can't solve it alone.
- **Risking erasure** — Aldermere's fate is their cautionary tale.
- **Crossing paths via triggers** — not a resistance guild HQ the party stumbles into Session 2.

For future scenes introduce contacts deliberately. Session 2 centers on Corbin, one honest lieutenant, residents and the collector's operation. The profiles below remain future options, not a second scheduled cast.

## NPC: Captain Beatriz de Montal *(ADJUSTABLE)*

**INVENTED — Far Mariner, Vorona**

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | Square-jawed marine captain, grey Vorona coat, **incorruptible** reputation |
| **Secret** | Was **Vesk Aldermere's last ally** — carried copies of ledger excerpts to a safe drop; Aldermere's hearing was **partly Beatriz's idea** ("daylight protects") |
| **Motivation** | Ash is a body. Someone murdered a civil servant in front of ten thousand witnesses. |
| **Fear** | Transferred to Harbor Crown after the Reckoning; knows two prior investigators **vanished** mid-inquiry |
| **Stat block** | Not needed until combat — treat as skilled veteran (Level 2–3 defender flavor) |

### How the PCs cross paths

| Trigger | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| **Session 1 Scene 4** — party reports to "the Watch" | Optional: they meet a **Revels Guard** stonewall first; if they **persist** or name Aldermere, a **Vorona lieutenant** overhears and sends them to Beatriz "off the record" — or Beatriz finds **them** at their lodging that night |
| **Renown hook** — "folk who were at the Reckoning" | Beatriz attends the same public house; buys a round; *"I need to know what you saw. Not for my report — I don't have one."* |
| **Demona mentions psionic strings** | Beatriz goes still: *"She told me someone was steering from far away. I didn't believe her until today."* |
| **Party praised in a Font broadsheet** | Beatriz uses article as excuse to approach without tail |

### What she offers / needs

- **Offers:** Harbor Crown safehouse once; names of **two missing clerks**; map of which districts **never** file missing persons.
- **Needs:** **Evidence that isn't ash** — paper, witnesses the memory fuzz hasn't taken, or a cult reliquary.

### Pitfall

Don't make Beatriz a **replacement PC** or deus ex machina. She can **not** arrest Dury. She **can** get the party beaten by Vorona politics if they name her publicly.

| Trigger | Scene |

### Pitfall

Don't force Pippa if table doesn't engage. She's **offer**, not obligation.

## What resistance is NOT

- **Not a unified faction** — no "Resistance HQ" unless late Act earns it.
- **Not a shortcut around evidence and jurisdiction**; Dury's protection is political, not automatic invulnerability.
- **Not all good** — Inān and Belisario have agendas; Iverholt cares about souls not peasants.

---

## Session-building checklist

When adding a resistance beat:

1. **One NPC** from this doc, max two if players are alliance-building.
2. **One concrete offer, one concrete need** — trade, not gift.
3. **One risk** — relay targeting, transfer, memory fuzz, Alvaro surveillance.
4. Tie to **renown** from Session 1 group title — they're interesting because they **witnessed** the Reckoning.
