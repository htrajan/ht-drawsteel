# The Quiet Resistance

> **Tags:** INVENTED NPCs placed on CANON factions (Far Mariners, the Font, House Navarr, Bureau of Accounts).
>
> **Use when:** The party tries to involve authorities, seeks allies after the Reckoning, or you want a side character to **become important** (Lesley's request).

---

## Design intent

Capital's rot is **systemic** — the party should **feel** that reporting crimes fails before they find people who **already knew** and kept fighting anyway. Resistance NPCs are:

- **Competent but constrained** — they can't solve it alone.
- **Risking erasure** — Aldermere's fate is their cautionary tale.
- **Crossing paths via triggers** — not a resistance guild HQ the party stumbles into Session 2.

Introduce **one** resistance thread per arc unless players aggressively seek more.

---

## Faction overview

| Faction | Resistance role | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Far Mariners (Vorona)** | Honest captains who treat ash as murder | Transfers, political pressure, no jurisdiction in Gilded Ward |
| **The Font / Paperfeathers** | Document what broadsheets won't print | Censorship, libel law, Fulcrum advertising leverage |
| **House Navarr censors** | Hunt **heresy** (cult), not ministerial graft | Won't touch Dury without church-political cover |
| **Bureau of Accounts** | Numbers don't lie — if someone lives to file them | Aldermere dead; successors watched |
| **Imperial University (Alvaro)** | Theoretical knowledge of psionic margins | Academics don't fight; Actian alumni may spy *on* the party |

## NPC: Captain Sera Marche *(ADJUSTABLE)*

**INVENTED — Far Mariner, Vorona**

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | Square-jawed marine captain, grey Vorona coat, **incorruptible** reputation |
| **Secret** | Was **Vesk Aldermere's last ally** — carried copies of ledger excerpts to a safe drop; Aldermere's hearing was **partly Marche's idea** ("daylight protects") |
| **Motivation** | Ash is a body. Someone murdered a civil servant in front of ten thousand witnesses. |
| **Fear** | Transferred to Harbor Crown after the Reckoning; knows two prior investigators **vanished** mid-inquiry |
| **Stat block** | Not needed until combat — treat as skilled veteran (Level 2–3 defender flavor) |

### How the PCs cross paths

| Trigger | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| **Session 1 Scene 4** — party reports to "the Watch" | Optional: they meet a **Revels Guard** stonewall first; if they **persist** or name Aldermere, a **Vorona lieutenant** overhears and sends them to Marche "off the record" — or Marche finds **them** at their lodging that night |
| **Renown hook** — "folk who were at the Reckoning" | Marche attends the same public house; buys a round; *"I need to know what you saw. Not for my report — I don't have one."* |
| **Demona mentions psionic strings** | Marche goes still: *"She told me someone was steering from far away. I didn't believe her until today."* |
| **Party praised in a Font broadsheet** | Marche uses article as excuse to approach without tail |

### What she offers / needs

- **Offers:** Harbor Crown safehouse once; names of **two missing clerks**; map of which districts **never** file missing persons.
- **Needs:** **Evidence that isn't ash** — paper, witnesses the memory fuzz hasn't taken, or a cult reliquary.

### Pitfall

Don't make Marche a **replacement PC** or deus ex machina. She can **not** arrest Dury. She **can** get the party beaten by Vorona politics if they name her publicly.

## NPC: Inān al-Adwiyya *(CANON name, INVENTED campaign role)*

**CANON** — Guildmaster of **the Font**, broadsheets, Paperfeathers (`The Basics.md`).

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | Sharp, wealthy, knows everything; prints **almost nothing** dangerous |
| **Secret** | Has a **private annex** of stories killed from print — "the morgue" — including three years of **unexplained disappearances** near revel sites |
| **Motivation** | Information is power; succession war will need a **narrative**; she won't hand the wrong side a weapon |
| **Relationship to party** | **Evaluator** — are they useful, controllable, or firewood? |

### How the PCs cross paths

| Trigger | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| **Group title / Reckoning renown** | A Paperfeather delivers a **free broadsheet** with the party's names misspelled — invitation to Font offices "for correction" |
| **Mac (Grifter)** social play | Inān appreciates a con artist; test negotiation per Draw Steel rules |
| **Party recovers Kell's note or cult pamphlet** | She wants **exclusive**; offers access to morgue files in trade |
| **Party harms a Paperfeather** | She cuts them off — see **Mira** below |

### What she offers / needs

- **Offers:** Redacted morgue clippings; **Mira** as field asset; warning when a broadsheet **plants** false story about them.
- **Needs:** **Proof** before she prints — ash isn't enough for libel law; wants **names on ledgers** or cult regalia.

## NPC: Mira Tallow *(ADJUSTABLE)* — Paperfeather

**INVENTED — side character → main character hook (Lesley)**

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | Fifteen, fast, ink-stained fingers, sells broadsheets on Canal Verge |
| **Secret** | Grandmother **attended the Gilded Lantern** the night of the fire-that-wasn't; kept a **ticket stub**; grandmother now **forgets** she was there — memory fuzz |
| **Motivation** | Prove grandmother isn't senile; find what the city **unwrote** |
| **Psionic tie** | Not psionic herself — but **felt** the Amphitheater panic as *"like when Gran can't remember my name"* |

### How the PCs cross paths

| Trigger | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| **Session 1 cold open** | Optional texture — bumps into party selling handbills about **the Reckoning**; no name yet |
| **After Reckoning renown** | Recognizes them from handbill illustration; follows Demona **because she felt something** during the crowd panic |
| **Inān sends them to morgue** | Mira is the runner who unlocks the side door; overhears; *"My gran had ash on her shoes once. She said it was confetti."* |
| **Dorian Layer 1 reveal** | Mira's stub **dates** contradict official fire record — first **paper** proof |

### Escalation (organic)

- Session 3–4: Mira's grandmother **starts forgetting Mira** — relay fuzz or Author bleed.
- Mid-Act: Mira becomes **relay target** — rescue scene, Demona spotlight.
- Late Act: Mira chooses **Font byline** or **party** — Lesley's "side character becomes main."

### Pitfall

Don't force Mira if table doesn't engage. She's **offer**, not obligation.

## NPC: Censor-Lector Thane Voss *(ADJUSTABLE)*

**INVENTED — House Navarr, Church of Saint Ysabella the Pitiless**

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | Cold, precise priest-censor; investigates **doctrine violations** |
| **Secret** | Has seen **corruption damage** on three cultists (Session 1 stat block flavor) — recognizes **same stain** on archived heresy from 20 years ago |
| **Motivation** | The Author's work is **blasphemy against Creation** — unmaking souls Usurps the Pitiless's dominion over judgment |
| **Limit** | Won't help with **treasury fraud**; will help with **extraplanar cult** if party frames it as heresy |

### How the PCs cross paths

| Trigger | Scene |
| --- | --- |
| **Party loots cult regalia / holy weakness on Namefeeder** | Navarr wardens confiscate item; Voss interviews party |
| **Party seeks blessing or healing** | Cathedral encounter; Voss asks about **grey ash** on their boots |
| **Marche vouches for them** | Reluctant introduction — marines and censors don't trust each other |

### What he offers / needs

- **Offers:** **Holy water / consecration** resources vs voidlings; archive reference to **"Unwritten" pamphlet** burned 22 years ago; political **cover** as "heretic hunters" not "Dury investigators."
- **Needs:** Living cultist or **tear** sample; won't protect Minister Dury's reputation.

## What resistance is NOT

- **Not a unified faction** — no "Resistance HQ" unless late Act earns it.
- **Not able to topple Dury** without party stature + Aldermere-grade proof.
- **Not all good** — Inān and Kellan have agendas; Voss cares about souls not peasants.

---

## Session-building checklist

When adding a resistance beat:

1. **One NPC** from this doc, max two if players are alliance-building.
2. **One concrete offer, one concrete need** — trade, not gift.
3. **One risk** — relay targeting, transfer, memory fuzz, Alvaro surveillance.
4. Tie to **renown** from Session 1 group title — they're interesting because they **witnessed** the Reckoning.
