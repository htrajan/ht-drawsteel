# Dorian and the Vanished Audience

> **Player-owned history:** Dorian is a devil-kind revenant and a tactician. His audience vanished while he was on stage; bereaved people wrongly blamed and murdered him; he returned as a revenant.
>
> **Unresolved:** Why he alone remained, who commissioned the atrocity, how the Author's power works, and how Dorian might clear his name. Session 2 does not answer these questions.

## Preserve the distinction

The audience was erased. Dorian witnessed it, was subsequently murdered, and rose again. Do not describe him as having been erased and returned. A cultist can be mistaken about this, but the narration cannot turn that mistake into history.

Dorian recognized the old playhouse stage beneath the Grand Amphitheater during Session 1. That connects his past to Capital without resolving the cause of his survival. Preserve whichever details he actually shared with the other heroes.

## Gilded Lantern material

The **Gilded Lantern** is the playhouse name used in existing preparation. Its incorporation into the Amphitheater and Dorian's recognition belong to Session 1 continuity. Exact dates, seat count, a patron's motive, a play title, and who altered public records remain adjustable unless the player or table established them.

The old fire cover story and a playbill with other names scratched out can support future investigation. Use the playbill only if it was actually found or later encountered; do not add it to inventory because it appears in a run sheet.

## Knowledge boundary

| Established or player-owned | Still unresolved |
| --- | --- |
| Audience erased while Dorian was on stage | Why Dorian was not erased |
| Wrongful murder afterward by the bereaved | Whether the Author intended anything about Dorian's later fate |

| Recognition of the stage and erasure pattern in Capital | The full chain of perpetrators, commissioners, and cover-up |
| Kell and the Unwritten connection encountered in S1 | Whether Kell correctly understood his patron's intentions |

**Do not assert the discarded retained-witness explanation.** It was planned for an unplayed realm excursion and has been withdrawn from active preparation.

## Session 2 — The Price of Protection

Corbin's invitation leads to limited work with a Gold Buttons office, a patrol in the neglected Dog-leg, and a corrupt official's basement collection. Dorian can compare witness accounts, speak with residents, devise access to the house, protect evidence or direct the fight. None requires a new personal revelation.

The animated collection is unrelated to his audience's erasure unless future evidence establishes otherwise. It is not populated by stolen souls or alternate versions of anyone. The official closure of Vesk's case can resonate with Dorian's history without explaining it.

Gold Buttons and residents respond to his actions, not automatic guilt from his devil-kind revenant ancestry. Capital contains revenants; do not write every encounter as fear or disgust. He can refuse to disclose his private history without losing the job or reward.

## Later opportunities, not fixed revelations

- Recover a reliable record or witness contradicting the public cover story.
- Identify a person who commissioned, concealed, or profited from the old atrocity.
- Let Dorian decide whether public exoneration, vengeance, protection of others, or another goal matters most.
- Give evidence before explanations; do not preselect why he survived without the Director's approval.
- Do not impose an Author-created revenant origin, a fabricated past, or a required departure from Orden.
