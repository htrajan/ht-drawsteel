# Dorian and the Vanished Audience

> **SPOILER — Director layer.** Players should learn this in layers across Act 2 and the campaign. Do not dump this doc into read-aloud text.
>
> **Tags:** INVENTED (specific events) + CANON (Revenant ancestry, Amphitheater/playhouse geography from Session 1).

## One-sentence truth

**INVENTED:** Twenty-some years ago, at **The Gilded Lantern** playhouse on this same stage-footprint, cult **the Unwritten** helped the **Author** perform its first mass erasure in Capital — unmaking a full house of spectators mid-performance. **Dorian was not erased.** He stood alone on the boards as the house emptied. The bereaved — lovers, kin, and friends of the vanished — **wrongly blamed him** for the atrocity and **murdered him**. He rose again as a **revenant** (CANON: unjust death + vengeance). The playbill with only his name left is the city's **accusation made permanent**, not a failed erasure. Everything since is Dorian seeking the truth the mob denied him — and the Author watching the **witness who wouldn't stay dead**.

## The night of the vanished audience

**INVENTED — full sequence:**

### Before the show

- Dorian's troupe (tactician-turned-player arc — he directed from the stage) opened a **new work**, a meta-play about **forgotten names** — **ADJUSTABLE title:** *The Last Draft*.
- A **patron** in the minister's circle (not yet Dury — a predecessor) wanted a rival playwright **unwritten**. Cult Curator offered a **demonstration** at full house — maximum narrative "energy."
- **Relays** were planted in the ticket booth and backstage — same mechanic as Pell Quist, decades earlier.

### During the show

- Mid-act climax, the Author **committed full focus** through the thin place.
- Audience dissolved **from the edges in** — grey ash, cut-off screams — **identical** to Aldermere and Session 1 read-aloud.
- Dorian on stage saw it **from the light** — last man standing as the house emptied.

### After the erasure — the mob

**INVENTED:**

- Dorian on stage, ash settling, still holding a line no one will hear — **the only living soul in the building**.
- Word spreads before official lies do. **Bereaved** arrive: a merchant's wife, a soldier's father, a poet's lover — people who had someone in row seven, row twelve, the gallery. They find **grey dust** where their people sat and **one man standing in the lights**.
- They conclude what grief demands: **he did this**. Sorcery, devilry, a troubadour's curse — the mechanism doesn't matter to them. They **drag him down**, beat him, **kill him** on or just off the stage. Some are people he knew.

**Director note:** This is **wrongful accusation**, not justice. Dorian may have tried to explain; ash and panic read as guilt. Do not frame the bereaved as cartoon villains — they lost everyone in an instant and reached for the only face left.

### Dorian's death and return

**CANON mechanism** (`Revenant.md`): Revenants rise from **unjust death** and **burning desire for vengeance**, retaining memory and personality.

**INVENTED application:**

- Dorian **died by murder**, not by erasure. His death was **unjust** — he did not unmake the audience.
- His **unfinished business:** clear his name; learn **who really emptied the house**; finish what the mob cut short.
- He **woke** hours or days later (Director's call) — revenant flesh, memory intact, **misblamed** in every story that followed.

**The playbill (only Dorian's name):** **INVENTED** — After the official **fire** cover-up, surviving broadsheets and posted casts were altered or reprinted with every name **scratched out except Dorian's** — the city marking him **guilty** in ink. The Session 1 prop is that accusation preserved, not an Author bookmark. Cult Curator handwriting on the scratches is optional — they may have **leaned into** the false verdict.

### Aftermath (public record)

**INVENTED:**

- Official story: **fire** — tragic, no bodies recovered, playhouse later demolished "for safety" (it wasn't — it's **inside** the Amphitheater).
- Dorian fled Capital — ash on his cuffs from the **audience**, blood on his hands from the **mob** (or his own), **no proof** of the real horror, memory fuzzy on years immediately after (revenant trauma).
- He wandered Vasloria, eventually Broadhurst, joined the party — **Act 1** — still not knowing *where* it began, only *what* it looked like.
- Session 1 **proves** it began here.

## Why the Author cares about Dorian

**INVENTED:**

1. **The witness** — Dorian is the only person who **saw** the first mass erasure from the stage and **remembers**. That makes him a living record the Author didn't intend to leave.
2. **Narrative irony** — the city **blamed the wrong man** and killed him; the Author delights in miswritten stories. Dorian's return is a **second act** it didn't script.
3. **Anchor risk** — a revenant who remembers the audience may ** resist memory fuzz** around the Gilded Lantern event; the Author wants to study or silence that.
4. **Bait** — Dorian draws the party into the Author's preferred genre: **tragedy with a returning lead** wrongly accused.

**Important:** The cult **may believe** Dorian was once unwritten and returned — **doctrine error**. Kell can speak in those terms. The **Director truth** is: audience erased, Dorian murdered, Dorian revenant.

Session 1 factory trap was **curiosity**, not extermination. Kell: *"He said you would come."*

## Layered reveal schedule

Per [`Soul.md`](../Soul.md): prove the thread early; **resolve at stature**. Use **one reveal per arc**, not one per session.

### Layer 0 — Session 1 (done)

- Same erasure **of the audience**; same stage; Author's calling card; Kell names Dury and "Unwritten Dark."
- Dorian may reveal (Scene 4): he **witnessed** the first erasure from the stage; he was **wrongly murdered** afterward *(player canon)*.

### Layer 1 — Act 2 sessions 2–3 (Party)

**Triggers:** Renown after Reckoning; resistance NPC contact; Font broadsheet footnote.

**Reveal:** The Gilded Lantern **existed**; official records say **fire**; one Paperfeather kid has a **grandmother's ticket stub** with a date that doesn't match the fire report. Optional: a broadsheet morgue clipping shows Capital once **openly blamed Dorian** for the "fire."

**Dorian gets:** Proof it was **covered up** civically, not just magically — and that the city **already lied about him once**.

### Layer 2 — Act 2 mid (Party)

**Triggers:** Recovered Aldermere evidence; cult document from second cell; Demona touches old relay residue in archives.

**Reveal:** The massacre was a **commissioned demonstration** for a **Ministry of Revels** official; cult name **the Unwritten**; relays are **old technology**.

**Dorian gets:** **Political** chain — not necessarily Dury by name yet, but **Ministry → cult → Author**.

### Layer 3 — Act 2 climax (Party)

**Triggers:** Confrontation with Dury's intermediary; or protected witness; or Author speaking through thin place.

**Reveal:** **Dury** (or his predecessor's pact Dury inherited) **renewed** the bargain; Dorian's play was chosen because the patron **hated the playwright** and wanted max crowd.

**Dorian gets:** **Who to hate** that's reachable in Act 2 finale — still not the Author itself.

### Layer 4 — Act 3 / campaign (Director → Party)

**Triggers:** Timescape lore; direct Author contact; revenant saint or Null mentor; surviving bereaved who finally learn the truth.

**Reveal:** Full chain — **Author erased the audience**; **mob murdered Dorian**; **city and cult** both wrote him as villain. Confronting the Author means confronting the erasure; **exoneration** may require public proof on the Amphitheater stage (symbolic or literal final performance).

**Dorian gets:** **Origin payoff** — clear his name, name the true guilty, choose whether vengeance includes the **wrongful killers**, the **commissioner**, or the **Author** — or **end the story** on his terms.
