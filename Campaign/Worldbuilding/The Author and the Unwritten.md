## What the Author is (Director layer)

**INVENTED:**

The Author is a **remote psionic intelligence** — not a god, not a devil, not a saint's intercessor. It exists **beyond the ordered narrative of the manifolds**, in a region cultists call **the Unwritten Dark**: the blank margin where stories haven't been written yet, or have been **cut**.

It experiences reality as **draft text**. People are characters; cities are settings; erasure is **deletion** — not death, which leaves a corpse and a memory, but **revision**.

It cannot fully enter Orden. It ** presses through thin places** — stages, archives, factories where meaning is made — via cult ritual and **relays**.

**CANON grounding:** Psionics "warp reality with the mind" (`The Basics.md`). The timescape includes planes where identity dissolves — the Abyssal Waste's **lethe**, the World Below's exiled horrors. The Author is **in that family of ideas** without being a named book creature. Treat it as a **campaign-original void power** until/unless the table imports a specific monster.

## What the Author wants

**INVENTED — drives all Act 2+ villainy:**

1. **Feed on erasure.** Each unmaking is sustenance and art.
2. **Edit Capital's story.** A city of playwrights and spies is a perfect workshop — narratives are currency here.
3. **Cultivate interesting exceptions.** Dorian is the prime specimen: the **witness** on stage when the house was edited — **not erased himself** — who **died anyway** (wrongfully murdered) and **returned**.
4. **Expand the margin.** Long game: more thin places, more relays, more of the Unwritten Dark bleeding into Orden (Timescape / Mac thread).

The Author is **curious** about the party after Session 1 — not rushing to kill them. It prefers **audience**.

## Cult: the Unwritten

**INVENTED:**

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Public face** | None. Cells hide as archivist societies, playwright salons, "revisionist" literary clubs |
| **Doctrine** | Existence is a first draft; the Author will **revise** the world into perfection; the unwritten are not murdered — they are **edited out** |
| **Structure** | Decentralized **Curators** (cell leaders) like **Vharos Kell**; higher ranks called **Editors** and **Prologues** (Act 2 endgame hooks) |
| **Recruitment** | Disillusioned scholars, failed playwrights, clerks who've seen too much ledger fraud and want **clean pages** |
| **Patron pipeline** | Powerful clients (Minister Dury, others unnamed) pay in **names, funds, and silence** — not always knowing the full cost |

**Session 1 cell:** Levitating Carpet Spooling Factory — **Vharos Kell, the Curator**. Former city archivist. Factory chosen for **warp-threads and vertical space** (good tear anchor) and distance from Gilded Ward eyes.

**After Session 1:** Cell broken but Author intact. Other cells remain. Kell's death or capture **does not** stop erasures.

## Minister Corvane Dury's connection

**INVENTED — Director layer:**

Dury is **not** the Author. He is a **client**.

Years ago, rising through Revels ministry, he found ledger ghosts — people who'd been paid twice and then **never existed**. He traced whispers to a salon that offered **discretion beyond murder**.

He made a bargain: **unwrite my enemies, unwrite my mistakes.** The cult handled disposal; he handled **permits, guards, and story**.

He now understands the Author **uses him** as a pen — and he can't put it down without being unwritten himself. Aldermere was about to name him **with documents**, not rumor — that threatened the whole arrangement.

**Public layer:** Charming minister, civic hero of festivals.

**Party layer (after Session 1):** Name dropped by Kell; political mountain.

**Director layer:** Dury may not know where the Author "lives"; he knows how to **request** an erasure through Curator channels.

## Rules of erasure (consistency for all sessions)

Agents **must** keep these rules stable.

### The unmaking

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Effect** | Target dissolves from edges inward into **grey ash**; scream often cuts off mid-sound |
| **Memory** | **INVENTED — harsh default:** Mortals who weren't psionically shielded **forget** the unmade person over days unless they wrote the name down *before* the erasure. Demona and strong-willed witnesses ** resist** fuzzing (Intuition test to hold a name). |
| **Evidence** | Ash remains unless wind/scattered; **objects** the person carried may persist (Pell's note) |
| **No save at full power** | When the Author acts **directly** with full focus (Aldermere on stage), **no roll interrupts** — Session 1 rule stands |
| **Incomplete ritual** | Kell's "Strike the Scene" — psychic damage and weakened, **not** full erasure. Local cult lacks Author's full bandwidth |

### Tells (Demona / psionics)

- **Cold, vast mind** — not spell-shaped; **mind-shaped**
- **Stage-dust and cold** smell at thin places
- **Hollow white mask** imagery in mental contact
- **Spiral of un-written text** — letters peeling off a page into grey
- **Resonance thread** after an erasure — leads toward **relays** for minutes, not hours

### Relays

**INVENTED — Session 1 mechanic, campaign-wide:**

A **relay** is a living person turned into an **antenna** for the Author's signal during a remote erasure.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Creation** | Author or Curator psion primes a susceptible mind (often without victim's knowledge) |
| **Behavior** | Moves "wrong" — too smooth, puppeted; flees toward extraction or tear |
| **End** | Connection severs → victim **unmade** instantly (Pell Quist) |
| **Demona read** | Success/partial chase = clean read of strings; failure = ash only |
| **Network** | Multiple relays exist citywide; Act 2–3 thread for Lesley |

### Thin places / tears

- **Old playhouse stage** (now Amphitheater center) — **strongest** Capital anchor; Dorian's night
- **Archives, factories, rehearsal halls** where "drafts" are made
- **Blank tear** — ritual slit of grey nothing; extraplanar minions (Namefeeders, Unmade Wisps); can be **pinched shut** (Demona, creative disruption)

### Limits (why the party still lives)

- Author acts **remotely** in Act 2; full unmaking costs **focus + relay infrastructure**
- It **wants** Dorian studied, not deleted yet
- Closing tears **hurts** local cells but doesn't kill the Author
- **Holy damage** punishes voidlings (Namefeeder, Wisps) — CANON-adjacent weakness on custom creatures

## Connection to Dorian

**Summary — full detail in [`Dorian and the Vanished Audience.md`](Dorian%20and%20the%20Vanished%20Audience.md):**

- The playhouse erasure was the Author's **first successful mass revision** in Capital — **the audience only**, not Dorian.
- Dorian **watched from the stage**, was **murdered by bereaved** who wrongly blamed him, and **returned** as a revenant (CANON mechanism: unjust death + vengeance).
- The playbill with only his name is the city's **false accusation**, not an Author bookmark. The cult may **mistakenly** treat him as someone who was unwritten and came back.
- Session 1: Author **notices** Dorian again via Kell's trap. Mask and playbill are **calling cards**.

**Why Kell may say "wouldn't stay unwritten":** Cult doctrine assumes all who "survive" an editing are unwritten souls. Dorian **was never erased** — Kell is wrong, or speaking in cult jargon for "wouldn't stay **written out of the story**." The Author's interest is the **witness who wouldn't stay dead**.

## Calling cards and signatures

Use sparingly:

- Smooth **white half-mask**, faceless
- Yellowed **playbill** with names scratched out — one left legible (Dorian)
- Notes in perfect calligraphy: *"Come alone to receive your reward"*
- Ash piles with **no heat**, no scorch marks on stone
- Factory **blank tear** — grey slit, cold air, stage-dust smell

## Pitfalls for agents

- Don't let players "save" a full-power erasure mid-sentence — **inevitability is the point** when the Author commits fully.
- Kell is a **zealot**, not the mastermind — monologue fodder, not final boss.
- Dury is **protected politically** until the party has standing — don't arrest him Session 2 unless the table explicitly accelerates.
- Keep **Author / Impresario / Unwritten Dark** as one entity unless the Director splits them later.
