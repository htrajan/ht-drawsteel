# Campaign Worldbuilding — Index

> **Audience:** Future AI agents building Session documents for this campaign.
>
> **Purpose:** This folder is the series bible for Act 2 ("Corruption in Capital") and beyond. It grounds invented lore in Draw Steel canon and keeps the campaign's central mysteries coherent across sessions.

---

## How to use this folder

1. **Before writing a session**, read [`Campaign/Soul.md`](../Soul.md) (meta-rules) and [`Campaign/Narrative.md`](../Narrative.md) (story spine).
2. **Check this index** for which doc covers what you need.
3. **Use [`NPC and Faction Directory.md`](NPC%20and%20Faction%20Directory.md)** as your first stop when populating a scene with named figures.
4. **Extend, don't contradict.** New facts should fit the canon-vs-invented labels below. If you must override something, mark it *Director-adjustable* and note why.

---

## Canon vs. invented

Every doc in this folder tags facts as one of:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **CANON** | From official Draw Steel rules (`Rules/Chapters/The Basics.md`, ancestries, etc.). Do not change without explicit Director approval. |
| **INVENTED** | Created for this campaign. Consistent across all future sessions unless the Director retcons. |
| **ADJUSTABLE** | Invented but explicitly flagged as a suggestion — names, dates, or details the table may swap. |

When in doubt, prefer **CANON** for geography and great-house politics; prefer **INVENTED** for the Author, the Unwritten cult, and Dorian's specific backstory.

---

## Reveal layering (spoiler policy)

Session docs are run sheets for the table. Worldbuilding docs may contain secrets players don't know yet.

| Layer | Who knows | Use in sessions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Anyone in Capital could believe or rumor this | Safe read-aloud, rumor mill, NPC dialogue |
| **Party** | What the heroes have learned in play | Only facts established at the table |
| **Director** | Full truth in these docs | Foreshadowing, private beats, long-game payoffs |

**Rules for agents:**

- Do **not** dump Director-layer secrets into read-aloud text.
- Per [`Soul.md`](../Soul.md): defer Dorian's full origin payoff until the party has stature; early sessions *prove the thread is alive*, they don't close it.
- Demona's psionics and the relay/erasure mechanics should stay **consistent** with [`The Author and the Unwritten.md`](The%20Author%20and%20the%20Unwritten.md).

## Act 2 spine (one paragraph)

The heroes arrive in Capital as nobodies and witness **Vesk Aldermere** publicly unmade before she can name **Minister Corvane Dury**. The trail leads to cult **the Unwritten**, serving a psionic void-intelligence (**the Author / Impresario**) that erases people from existence. Dorian recognizes the horror from the night his audience vanished — at this same site, when it was a small playhouse. The Act's arc is rising from anonymity to civic standing strong enough to touch Dury and eventually the Author, while Demona's psionics unravel the relay network and the party navigates a city whose guards look away because the system is broken, the succession war is frozen, and whistleblowers become ash.
