## Current Session 2 spine

Corbin's initial private conversation establishes both the attempted cover-up and the reason for civilian recruitment. The Prince's death without an heir has intensified the struggle to rule; in this campaign, more partisan street fights occupy patrols while ordinary complaints go unanswered. With no annual increase in Prince's seals, the office cannot simply expand its sworn force. Corbin explains how civilian helpers can take complaints and mediate disputes, freeing officers for violence and arrests—and gaining the heroes access to open reports. At the **local Gold Buttons office**, the returning patrol confirms his account; Hanae handles permissions and payment without repeating the background. The heroes encounter suppression of Vesk's file themselves. Five assignments contain one fair, discoverable connection to her request for repeated complaints about supposedly completed repairs.

A routine Dog-leg patrol reveals a missing pump and money used for affluent clients' improvements. Residents blame **Commissioner Ottaviano Bellafonte**, an invented appointed representative rather than a claimed canonical MP. His public office leads to a private meeting with broker **Rudiger Fenwick** in his basement collection.

Investigation and RP can win access, copies, witnesses and tactical advantages. Once discovered or confronted, Bellafonte attempts to animate the collection and flee. Initiative begins before activation; clever prevention and capture remain real possibilities. Four custom hostile profiles, sixteen minions, Victory-adjusted budgets and a five-round charge clock keep one substantial battle focused.

The intended aftermath cancels access and sacks Corbin. Vesk's case becomes **“unexplained disappearance” without a public announcement**. Preserve the party's evidence, captures, public disclosures and genuine interventions. Other institutions remain available.

**Rewards:** 1 investigation Victory + 2 encounter Victories = **3 maximum**; verified civic-fraud evidence earns **+1 Wealth per hero**, with an earned payment surviving cancellation. Award only after actual completion. No XP conversion, free healing, character edits or app import.

## Knowledge safeguards

- Dury's involvement remains hidden. Director chain: Bellafonte → Fenwick → Civic Loomworks → Baldo Merovanni → Dury.
- Published text supports the Prince's death three years ago. The recruiting office, commissioner and financial scheme are invented.
- The Dog-leg is identified as poor by a secondary Chain reference; no verified single-poorest ranking.
- No retroactive factory guards.
- No Author body/location reveal or parallel realm.
- No imposed cause of Demona's disgrace or Dorian's survival.
- No omniscient Corbin or automatic escape because the outline expects it.
- Read-aloud stays observable, dialogue uses plain language, and preparation stays distinct from recorded outcomes.

Superseded patron, quiet-investigation, auction and realm drafts are preserved outside Campaign in Drafts/Superseded. They impose no current events or future destiny.
