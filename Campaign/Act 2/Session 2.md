> **Prepared, not played.** A 150-minute continuation in Capital: Corbin's warning, a chance to work inside the law, five open assignments, a neglected district, and a collector's private meeting that becomes a construct battle. Player-facing dialogue and read-aloud are in italicized blockquotes; the assignment table is a player handout. Other text is for the Director.

## Scene 1 — Corbin's invitation (0:00–0:15)

### Breakfast first

Use the established tavern and keeper. The heroes returned exhausted and slept; no factory replay or respite.

>
> *“This came for you this morning. The runner said to let you sleep.”*

### Why Corbin suggests deputy work

Give these beats as part of this private conversation, with room for questions between them. They are the explanation for the offer, not optional lore behind a successful test.

**The work he can offer:**

> *“There is a way to get you inside the office. We're paying civilians to help with complaints and patrols. It isn't detective work, but you'd be able to talk to the clerks and read the open reports.”*
>
> *“The Prince died three years ago. Forty-one years old, and no heir. Four houses and three guilds want to decide who rules next. People pick a side. Lately, more of them have been picking fights.”*
>
> *“Yesterday a patrol spent the afternoon breaking up a fight at the market. They were supposed to be following up thefts. Those reports are still waiting.”*

**Why the office cannot simply add guards:**

> *He takes a metal seal from its cord and turns the stamped dragon toward them.*
>
> *“The Prince's seal. Every sworn guard carries one. The Prince used to approve more each year. That stopped when he died. We can fill an empty post, but we can't just add another patrol.”*
>
> *“That's why we're taking on civilian help. Hear the complaints. Help people settle a quarrel before it becomes another fight. Bring back what needs an officer, with enough detail that we can act.”*

**Why he is asking them:**

> *“You could do some good. And while you're there, ask about Vesk. See whether they'll take your statement.”*
>
> *“Lieutenant Petrovic handles the applicants. She'll tell you what the work allows. I can't promise access to the murder file.”*

**Responses to likely questions:**

- **“Is Vesk's case just waiting like the theft reports?”**

  > *“No. I've been told not to take more statements. Not to wait until there's time. To stop. That's not how we handle a case we're too busy to finish.”*

- **“Why not hire more private guards?”**

  > *“Their employer pays them to guard his property. Their charter tells them what they're allowed to do. That doesn't make them our officers. We need someone to hear the complaints outside those guarded doorways.”*

- **“Who else could help?”**

  > *“The Gold Buttons enforce the law here on the Stays. Other districts have their own guards. Vorona's Far Mariners are the closest thing Capital has to a citywide force, but they don't command us. I can't order them to take over our cases.”*

- **“Did the houses kill the Prince?”**

**Director:** The staffing problem is real; it does not excuse the cover-up. These specific disturbances and the deputy program are campaign inventions; source distinctions are at the top of the sheet. Keep the full breakfast and private conversation inside fifteen minutes.

### What “citizen deputy” actually grants

**If they ask how far the letter reaches:**

**If they want work without joining:**

Declining loses faster access, not the adventure. Do not later revoke letters they never accepted.

## Scene 5 — The commissioner's other office (1:05–1:25)

### Choose an approach

The house is in Foxes on the Stays. The work letter is not a search warrant. Follow the heroes' plan:

| Approach | What makes it credible | Benefit and risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enter openly | Collection appointment, offer of a curio, restoration work, or accepted private delivery of repair evidence | Reach the gallery with a guide; the men know their faces. |
| Follow a delivery | Help the restorer or carry a display | Rear doors and service stairs; useful work can earn unescorted access. |
| Observe, then enter | Courtyard lightwell, service stair, disguise or an invited ally | Hear the meeting and locate the controls before revealing themselves. |
| Force entry | Break the rear doors or demand surrender | Immediate confrontation; Fenwick reaches for the case and Bellafonte for the controls. |

**Uncertain actions only:** Medium test, relevant characteristic and skill (+2): ≤11 fails, with an offered success-at-cost if useful; 12–16 succeeds with a stated complication; 17+ succeeds cleanly. Natural 19–20 improves the result and adds a reward, such as an unlatched escape door. At most one entry test and one positioning test, not a mandatory Sneak roll per hero. Assistance needs a different relevant skill and credible help: ≤11 bane, 12–16 edge, 17+ double edge.

State stakes first. Complications include an escort, recognition, or entry at the less useful staircase. Failure leaves other approaches open and does not erase overheard information.

## Scene 6 — A collection in motion (1:25–2:15)

### Stakes and ending

### Controls, terrain and escape — complete scenario rules

**The red lever:** Size 1T object, Stamina 20, no immunity or weakness; reachable at S4. It is exposed, not a hidden puzzle. Adjacent creature: maneuver to pull while idle; main action to jam/remove the handle with appropriate tools, automatically if uninterrupted before combat. In combat an adjacent hero can use a main action and a **medium Might/Reason test**: ≤11 no disable; 12–16 disable but take 3 damage from the spring; 17+ disable cleanly. Attacks may simply destroy it. A disabled lever cannot activate the collection. Grabbing the commissioner alone does not disable the lever, but prevents him reaching it. **Once running, breaking or jamming it prevents restart but does not stop the charge already in the Marshal.** Explain this before a player spends an action; visible rods and the rewinding gear establish why.

**The Marshal's drive:** Its heart mechanism is the combat objective, represented by the Marshal's Stamina, not a separate unannounced health bar. No additional protection against a hero's applicable suppression, sabotage or power. If an ability can shut down this magical construct under its actual rules, apply it; don't invent immunity. Its other actions still work if an effect suppresses only one named feature.

**Displays:** A hero adjacent to a case section can tip it with a main action. Choose a 3 × 1 line extending from that section, no roll: creatures there take 4 damage and are pushed 1 away; then the section is spent and the line is difficult terrain. Stability applies. An attendant caught in the line counts separately for area damage. A PC can break an ordinary case to take a small evidence page without an additional theft puzzle.

**Chandelier:** Centered J9, height 3; chain winch at H9. Adjacent creature spends a main action to release it: a 3 cube on the floor centered J9 deals 6 damage and knocks targets prone, then becomes difficult terrain. One use. Warn anyone standing below; players choose whether to drop it on occupied squares. These helpful terrain features carry no separate enemy EV.

**Evidence:** Fenwick's case starts open on Q3. Taking it or a packet is a maneuver adjacent to it; copying one relevant page takes a main action without a roll if uninterrupted. A secured copy elsewhere is never destroyed by an event in this room. The commissioner carries no secret extra archive.

**Civilian steward:** No combat turn. If warned, leave via the nearest safe staircase at the end of the current round; if a route is blocked, shelter behind cover until heroes clear it. No hostile attacks target them deliberately.

**Fugitives, not extra opponents:** Bellafonte and Fenwick each have Stamina 20, size 1M, speed 5, stability 0, Might +0, Agility +1, Reason +2, Intuition +1, Presence +2; no immunities, attacks, free strikes, spells, reactions or villain actions. They take a move, a maneuver and a main action normally but use them only to flee, handle objects, talk or escape a grab. Their inclusion does not increase hostile EV. If a hero tackles one, use the hero's Grab rules against these characteristics; a captive cannot pass through occupied spaces or barriers by fiat.

### Core combat rules used here

- Heroes and enemy groups alternate; every creature acts once per round. Non-minions normally have a move, maneuver and main action, and one triggered action per round. A main action can buy another move or maneuver.
- **Malice:** Start with average current Victories, rounded down; add living heroes plus round number at each round's start (normally +5/+6/+7/+8/+9). Spend only on options printed below. No separate hidden faction Malice list.
- **Power rolls:** 2d10 + listed modifier; ≤11 / 12–16 / 17+ are tiers 1/2/3. A natural 19–20 makes an ability tier 3 and grants an additional main action. Edge adds 2, bane subtracts 2; two edges/banes instead raise/lower outcome one tier. Edges and banes cancel before applying.
- **Potency:** M/A/R/I/P mean characteristics. “M < 2” applies its effect only below Might 2. **EoT** ends at the end of the affected creature's next turn, or current turn if applied during it. **Save ends:** d10 at each turn's end; 6+ ends the effect.
- **Prone:** Bane on strikes; melee strikes against you gain an edge; crawling costs an extra square. Stand Up is a maneuver. **Slowed:** Speed 2; cannot shift. **Grabbed:** Speed 0, cannot Knockback or be forcibly moved except by the grabber; bane on abilities not targeting the grabber. Movement separating the pair or teleporting ends the grab. The grabber moves at half speed if dragging a creature its size or larger.
- **Grab:** Maneuver, Melee/Weapon, melee 1, one creature. Roll Might: ≤11 no effect; 12–16 grab, but the target may first make a melee free strike; 17+ grabbed. Normally target your size or smaller; Might 2+ permits targets up to a size equal to Might. Normally hold only one creature. The two fugitives have no free strike, so tier 2 still captures them without damage.
- **Escape Grab:** Maneuver, self; roll Might or Agility, with a bane if smaller than the grabber: ≤11 no effect; 12–16 escape but the grabber may first make a melee free strike; 17+ escape cleanly. Fugitives use their printed characteristics and this action, not automatic escapes.
- **Recoveries:** Catch Breath is a maneuver, spending one of the hero's own Recoveries to regain recovery value; a dying hero cannot use it in combat. Outside combat, heroes may spend their remaining Recoveries normally. No free healing between scenes.
- **Free strikes:** These monsters deal listed damage without a roll; melee 1/ranged 5 or their signature distance, whichever is greater. An adjacent enemy willingly moving away without shifting/teleporting provokes a melee opportunity attack; forced movement does not. A monster with a bane against that target cannot make that opportunity attack.
- **Terrain:** Cover imposes a bane on damaging abilities when an intervening solid object blocks at least half a target. Difficult terrain costs +1 movement. Stability reduces horizontal forced movement, minimum 0. A fall of 2+ squares deals 2 damage per square and leaves the creature prone; reduce effective height by Agility. Ordinary climbing costs +1 movement per square unless a climb speed applies.
- Reducing an NPC to 0 can knock them unconscious rather than kill them. Deactivated automata stay down; no second phase.

**Minions inline:** Four separate squads, each **32 Stamina** (four × 8). A squad shares one attack roll; each target takes one result, plus Free Strike damage for each extra attacker, maximum three minions per target. A minion takes a move and main action, move and maneuver, or two moves—not all three. A squad critical gives each participating minion another main action, resolved together under the same squad rules.

Damage reduces the squad pool; remove a minion for every 8 lost. Non-area overflow removes additional nearest squadmates. Area damage is applied per member in the area, capped at 8 per affected member before immunity/weakness; ordinarily no casualties outside the area. Apply immunity/weakness once per source to the pool, last; this can change the number removed. Minions cannot regain Stamina, gain temporary Stamina or become winded. Simultaneous free strikes into one target combine into one strike.

Each squad has at most one named non-minion captain, whose Stamina is separate. Red's captain is the Marshal; blue's is the armor; green's is its first lion; yellow's is its lion if present. Captain and squad share an initiative group. **With Captain** ends when that captain falls; a surviving non-minion can assume command at the start of the next round.
