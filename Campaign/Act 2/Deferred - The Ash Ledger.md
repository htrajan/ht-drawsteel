## Opening — Melvin's secret approach

**Detective Melvin Corbin**, whom the heroes met during a return visit to the Grand Amphitheater, is Vesk Aldermere's lead investigator within the Fulcrum-chartered Ledger Lane Peacekeepers' Office.

His superiors order him to:

1. Record Vesk as a voluntary disappearance.
2. Return the factory cashbox as misfiled commercial property.
3. Approve a protective transfer for junior auditor **Oria Palomar** to a receiving detective who died last winter.

Melvin cannot resign or openly rebel without losing access. He waits for the heroes to identify him and establish discreet contact before showing them the contradictory docket and forged order.

The heroes have never met Oria when Melvin introduces her. She helped maintain Vesk's ciphered working books and can read the account abbreviations.

## Investigation structure

Use no more than one montage test. Combine counter-surveillance, evidence-annex entry, cashbox recovery, and discovery of Oria's route into that single montage or replace portions with direct infiltration.

Every result discovers:

- The factory was funded through **Civic Loomworks and Festooning**.
- Payments came from a Ministry of Revels emergency beautification fund.
- The authorization code also appears on reconstruction records for the supposed Gilded Lantern fire.
- The money routes through the dormant **Lantern Relief Account**.
- Oria's carriage will cross the Third Ledger Lift Bridge.

Failure changes alertness, evidence quality, and escort preparation; it never suppresses the route or core ledger connection.

---

## Oria Palomar and the forged transfer

Oria is the party's first living link to Vesk's private working methods. She remembers the account structure but has begun losing details of Vesk's face.

Her surviving casebook material establishes:

- Vesk tracked the Lantern Relief Account.
- Vesk met privately with Councillor **Sabine Corda**.
- Vesk wrote that Sabine “still cares how the record reads.”

A lift-bridge interception can be run as an objective combat: stop the carriage and free Oria before the end of round 4. Failure injures Oria or loses the original page; it does not kill her or remove the Sabine lead.

## Elodie Quintero and Demona's old network

**Elodie Quintero** was Demona's legislative scheduler and floor clerk before Demona's censure. She kept her position by becoming publicly quiet, not by causing Demona's disgrace.

Do not define or retcon the cause of Demona's disgrace. Invite one small player-supplied memory about Elodie and accept it as canon.

Elodie can establish:

- Sabine is custodian of Vesk's sealed memorandum.
- Sabine will grant ten private minutes after committee adjournment.
- Sabine protects exposed staff and cares about how her career is recorded.
- Suggesting she could profit from Dury's fall or seize his power will offend her.

## Blue-Wax cleanup

**Civic Loomworks and Festooning** employs deniable transport and records-cleanup crews. Their warrants carry warm blue wax and no Ministry insignia.

Use them as paid professionals rather than cult zealots. They flee, surrender, or bargain when an objective is lost.

A supernatural **Redactor** can support a later archive encounter. It destroys written names and blurs recent memory but cannot perform full erasure or speak for the Author.

Potential evidence packets:

1. **Factory grant and clerk signatures:** Supports Sabine's **Protection** motivation.
2. **Vesk's custodian designation:** Supports Sabine's **Legacy** motivation.
3. **Memorandum custody docket:** Raises the negotiation's starting Interest.

Evidence loss removes leverage, never the meeting or primary plot.
