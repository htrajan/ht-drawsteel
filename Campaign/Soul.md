# Overview

Treat this as a document of meta-rules on how to build this campaign: best practices on designing encounters, narrative beats, exploration montages, puzzles, and more. Append to this document to encode into memory any new rules and best practices we discover along the way.

## Player Requests

Here is what my players have indicated they desire in a campaign:

Wes (Dorian): 
-Good monsters and villains, he wants someone to hate
-Origin story payoff: was doing a performance then the audience got Thanos-sanpped. What happened?
-World reacts to your identity. Dorian is a corpse, Mac is a construct.

Lesley (Demona): 
-Look up what psionics means and make it more useful, perhaps out of combat
-Enjoys when a side character spontaneously becomes a main character

Miran (Keth):
-Likes having minions to use AOEs, in general planning out encounters to play to everyone’s strengths
-Make handing out titles more egalitarian rather than who landed the killing blow on the big boss, etc
-Maps with more tactical character — hiding places, debris, verticality, etc

Nick (Mac):
-His character has the Grifter complication
-Explore more of the lore of Vasloria / Orden / the Timescape

Aim for a meaningful combat per session -- in practice, there might be a meaningful combat in 8-9 of every 10 sessions.
