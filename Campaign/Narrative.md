# Synopses

## Act 1: The Delian Tomb (levels 1-3)

The heroes started in the town of Broadhurst and rallied to a cry for help to save the town blacksmith's daughter from goblins who had camped out in the Delian Tomb. In heeding this cry, they learned of the plottings of the Goblin Queen Bargnot and sought to acquire the primary artifact in the tomb (the cup of Iulius) before she did. Along the way, they gained further renown by dealing with other threats in the area such as the Forsaken Wraiths bandits, Water Wolves, a giant Ankheg, and a wizard who had taken some Dwarven merchants prisoner. They reached the final level of the Tomb before a rival party, the Gilded Hand, who was working in concert with the Queen. They acquired the cup after which point the Queen led a final desperate assault on Broadhurst where she was slain by the heroes. Having gained great renown for their deeds in Broadhurst, the heroes look to the city of Capital to continue their journey and grow their legend.
